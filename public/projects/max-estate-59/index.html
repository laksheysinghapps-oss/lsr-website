The concept

A bungalow's privacy, raised above the city

Max Estate 59 reimagines Lutyens' classical language for a vertical age. Each floor holds just three homes, open on their sides like a villa, with no wall shared with a neighbour. You arrive by one of two private lifts into your own lobby, while two service lifts keep the household's comings and goings apart.

Classical proportion, generous light and the quiet of a private home.
